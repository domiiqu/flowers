the inhabitant

he lives here. the world is the only thing you touch — turn the cold, bring the night, let it rain, tell him you slept badly — and he decides what to do about it. nothing here is wired into tending yet; the dials stand in for the day-book.

his draft
a prototype — nothing leaves this page. the drafts are canned.
how he works. a brain and a body, kept apart. the body is a fixed repertoire, drawn in code — wander, shiver, bundle up, open the umbrella, light the lantern, yawn, sleep, read. the brain is one function, decide(world, him), that looks at the world every second or so and picks from the repertoire. today it is a handful of rules; it is the one seam a model (jev, claude) would replace. the model never draws — it only chooses.
