the inhabitant

he lives here — a nook with a fire, a desk, a window on the field, and a door he uses when he feels like it. the world is the only thing you touch, and it follows today's page in the day-book; or take the dials and set it by hand. a text goes in the mailbox.

his draft
a prototype — nothing leaves this page. the drafts are canned.
how he works. a brain and a body, kept apart. the body is a fixed repertoire, drawn in code — wander, potter, sit by the fire, sit at the desk, look out the window, fetch the post, go in, go out, shiver, yawn, sleep, read. the brain is one function, decide(world, him), that looks at the world every second or so and picks from the repertoire. today it is a handful of rules; it is the one seam a model (jev, claude) would replace. the model never draws — it only chooses.
